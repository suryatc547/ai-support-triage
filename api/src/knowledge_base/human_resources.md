# Human Resources — Routing Policy

## Team: HR Team
**Contact:** hr@test.com
**Department:** Human Resources

## Scope of Support

Route any ticket to the HR Team when the email contains topics related to:

- **Onboarding**: New employee welcome, first day instructions, access setup requests from HR, documentation submission.
- **Offboarding**: Resignation processing, exit interviews, equipment return, final pay, relieving letters.
- **Profile Updates**: Name change, address update, emergency contact, bank account details update.
- **Leave & Attendance**: Leave application, leave balance queries, attendance correction, work-from-home approvals.
- **Payroll & Salary**: Salary queries, pay slip access, salary revision, payroll deductions, tax documents.
- **Benefits**: Medical insurance enrolment, health claims, travel allowance, employee benefits queries.
- **Training**: Training program enrollment, learning & development queries, mandatory compliance training.
- **Policy Clarifications**: Employee handbook questions, code of conduct, company policies.
- **ID & Access Cards**: Employee ID card issuance, replacement.
- **Performance & Transfers**: Performance review process, internal job transfers, promotion queries.

## Escalation Triggers

Route **immediately** if the email contains:
- "harassment", "discrimination", "hostile work environment" → Confidential HR escalation
- "resignation", "termination" → Urgent HR case
