# Facilities & Admin — Routing Policy

## Team: Facilities Team
**Contact:** facilities@test.com
**Department:** Facilities & Admin

## Scope of Support

Route any ticket to the Facilities Team when the email contains topics related to:

- **Building & Office Access**: Access card issues, lost access card, building entry problems, new access card requests.
- **Desk & Seating**: Hot desking, assigned desk requests, seating arrangement changes.
- **Maintenance**: Office repair requests, broken furniture, electrical issues, leaking taps, broken AC/heating.
- **Visitor Management**: Registering visitors, issuing visitor passes, guest Wi-Fi access.
- **Parking**: Parking pass requests, parking allocation issues.
- **Asset Allocation**: Company-provided furniture, office equipment, whiteboard, lockers.
- **Office Supplies**: Stationery, printer paper, toner cartridges.
- **Meeting Rooms**: Room booking conflicts, AV equipment in meeting rooms, room setup requests.
- **Relocation**: Moving desks or teams to a different floor or office area.
- **Cleaning & Hygiene**: Housekeeping requests, cleaning schedule issues.

## Escalation Triggers

Contact **Security Team** for:
- Unauthorized persons in restricted areas

Contact **IT Admin** for:
- AV/Tech equipment malfunctions in meeting rooms (e.g., projector, video conferencing)
